Why should we eat healthy food?

🥗

Real-Life Examples

Eating healthy foods protects our body from sickness and viruses, and gives us energy throughout the day. It also helps our mind stay sharp.

HEALTHY FOODS

Some Smart Choices

🥦

Vegetables

Vegetables can provide many important nutrients.

🍎

Fruits

Fruits provide vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients.

🌾

Whole Grains

Whole grains can provide carbohydrates and fiber.

Fast foods contain lots of fats and calories.

🍬

Candies have a lot of sugar that is not good for the body.
